About the hackathon

A national build sprint for real problems

Smart India Hackathon brings student teams together to solve problem statements sourced from government ministries, public-sector bodies, and industry partners — in one continuous build window, evaluated on working software, not slides.

Timeline

Six stages, one deadline

Stage 01

Problem statements released

Domain-tagged problem statements go live on the portal for teams to review.

Stage 02

Team registration opens

Teams of up to six register and lock in the problem statement they're solving.

Stage 03

Build window

Teams design, build, and test their prototype against the problem brief.

Stage 04

Project submission

Repo link, demo video, and writeup are submitted before the deadline.

Stage 05

Evaluation

A review panel scores each submission against the problem statement's goals.

Stage 06

Results announced

Winners and shortlisted teams are published on the results page.

Tracks

Six domains, one bar

Every problem statement sits in one of these tracks. Judging criteria stay consistent across all of them: working prototype, technical depth, and real-world feasibility.
